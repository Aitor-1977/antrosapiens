Dictamen Antropológico

A comprehensive synthesis of osteological findings, environmental context, and material culture correlations for Subject AX-2023-89B.

Based on the exhaustive examination of the skeletal remains recovered from Structure IV at Site 4, several definitive conclusions can be drawn regarding the individual's life history and circumstances of death. The state of preservation allows for a high degree of confidence in the subsequent osteological assessments.

1. Demography & Stature

Morphological features of the pelvis and cranium strongly indicate a biological female. Epiphyseal fusion and cranial suture obliteration suggest an age at death between 35 and 45 years. Estimated living stature, calculated via long bone measurement (femur maximum length: 412mm), is approximately 1.52m (± 4cm).

Key Finding

The robusticity of muscle attachments, particularly on the humeri and clavicles, indicates a lifetime of strenuous upper-body labor, unusual for the inferred social stratum based on burial context.

2. Pathology & Trauma

Examination revealed several instances of healed trauma and active pathology at the time of death. Most notably, a well-remodeled depressed fracture is present on the left parietal bone. The margins are smooth, indicating survival long after the initial event.

Dental health is notably poor, with advanced caries and significant alveolar resorption. This aligns with Hypothesis 1 (H1), corroborating the isotopic data that suggests a high-carbohydrate diet, likely maize-dependent, in the final years.

gavel Resolución Final

The evidence presented robustly supports the conclusion that the individual experienced significant lifestyle changes prior to mortality. The cause of death remains indeterminate, though systemic infection secondary to dental pathology is highly probable.
