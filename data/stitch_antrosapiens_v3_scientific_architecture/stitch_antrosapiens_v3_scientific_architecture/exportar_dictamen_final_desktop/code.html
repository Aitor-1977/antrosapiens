This document serves as the formal anthropological synthesis regarding the excavation site designated 'Alpha-7', situated in the lower river basin. The evidence collected between September 12th and November 4th, 2024, has undergone rigorous preliminary analysis.

I. Stratigraphic Context

The primary assemblage was recovered from Stratum III, characterized by highly compacted, humic-rich clay matrix. The uniform deposition suggests a period of stable occupation without significant environmental disruption. Associated macrobotanical remains indicate a reliance on cultivated flora typical of the middle holocene transition.

II. Material Culture Overview

Ceramic diagnostic sherds present a localized variant of the 'incised-punctate' tradition, showing significant technological continuity with previously established regional typologies, yet exhibiting novel formal attributes in vessel rim structures.
